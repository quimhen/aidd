# Consulting

AIDD is free and open source. If your team wants help adopting it, here's what's available.
**No pricing is published here** — every engagement is scoped to the team, so reach out and we'll
talk specifics.

## What's on offer

- **Agent workflow diagnosis** — a review of how your team currently works with AI coding agents,
  and a concrete proposal for where AIDD's pipeline (search → align → plan → tasks → build →
  converge) fits and what it changes.
- **AIDD implementation** — installing and configuring AIDD for your project: the Claude Code
  skill and hooks, the OpenCode/Codex/Gemini adapters your team actually uses, and integration with
  your existing tools (Git, issue tracker).
- **Custom rules and reporting** — planning rules, code conventions, and report formats specific
  to your team's stack and process, built on top of AIDD's own templates rather than replacing
  them.
- **Team training** — a hands-on session on working with AI agents through AIDD's pipeline:
  writing specs it can search, reading its intake/align output, running Step 6 audits for real.
- **Ongoing support** — ad hoc help as your team keeps using AIDD day to day.

## Get in touch

Open a [GitHub issue](../../issues/new) describing what you need, or start a
[discussion](../../discussions) if you're not sure yet which of the above fits. We'll follow up
from there.
